561: The truncation at second order nevertheless constitutes a closure assumption: for an arbitrary distribution, higher-order statistical contributions need not be small.
562: %
563: Retaining all orders would instead produce a hierarchy of statistical moments, analogous to a Carleman expansion \cite{Carleman1932}, which would itself require closure.
564: 
565: Here, we close the hierarchy at second order by assuming that the densities remain approximately Gaussian, so that cumulants of order three and higher are negligible and the remaining moments are determined by the mean and covariance.
